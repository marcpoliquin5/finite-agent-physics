# FINITE July 2026 submission checklist

This checklist maps FINITE to the published [challenge page](https://aibuilderschallenge-bob.bemyapp.com/)
and [official rules](https://res.cloudinary.com/ideation/image/upload/q_100%2Cf_pdf%2Cdpr_auto/id-ibm-skillsbuil-3eec69/pkqvg8j3q3a4teedy1kd.pdf).
The July entry is due **July 31, 2026 at 11:59 PM ET**. The selected theme is the
**Wildcard Challenge: Build Intelligent Systems for the Future of Work**.

An engineering check may be completed by reproducible repository evidence. Identity, enrollment,
registration, coursework, Bob use, account publication, consent, and submission checks require the
entrant. No agent may infer or fabricate them.

## Judging strategy

The published rubric describes four criteria scored from 1 to 5, for a maximum of 20 points.

| Criterion | Judge-visible FINITE proof | Credible 5/5 gate |
|---|---|---|
| Technical Execution | Typed compiler, dual admission, adaptive runtime, effects, verifier, MCP/API/console, release artifacts | One repeatable path; green gates; genuine Bob/Granite; no hidden failure or overclaim |
| Innovation | Time, provider quotas, physical resources, semantics, evidence, and effect authority share one envelope | Show refusal before spend and a residual plan changing while settled promises remain fixed |
| Challenge Fit | Framework-neutral control layer for consequential future-of-work workflows | Bob invokes FINITE and receives an actionable admitted/refused lifecycle on StormShift |
| Implementation & Feasibility | Durable local runtime, explicit adapter ABI, sealed replay, package/provenance checks, honest deployment boundary | Fresh clone and judge URL work; limitations are visible; no production or agency implication |

The fictional emergency-operations scenario makes deadline, accessibility, bilingual, grounding,
resource, and publication-safety promises legible in under three minutes. It must never imply
deployment by Miami-Dade County or any external agency.

## Recommended freeze order

1. Finish fair-benchmark and Production Survival validation and generate the local release candidate.
2. Publish the reviewed Bob-ready branch and let CI run at the exact commit.
3. Complete a substantive Bob work package and the same-run MCP lifecycle.
4. Run one live Granite task through FINITE and preserve the redacted receipt.
5. Configure judge access, verify the UI/API from the intended unauthenticated or shared account.
6. Freeze manifests, raw benchmark evidence, checksums, SBOM, provenance, and claim table.
7. Record, caption, and link-check the video against the immutable tag.
8. Complete the project page and submit early; retain every receipt.
